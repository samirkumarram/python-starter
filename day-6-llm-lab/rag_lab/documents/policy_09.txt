Policy ID: POL-009
Policy Name: Incident Reporting Policy

Purpose:
This document defines the organization's incident reporting policy.

Policy Rules:
Employees must report security incidents to the IT security team within 30 minutes of discovery.

Exceptions:
Critical incidents must be escalated immediately to the security manager.

Responsibility:
The relevant department is responsible for implementing
and monitoring this policy.

Review:
This policy must be reviewed annually.
