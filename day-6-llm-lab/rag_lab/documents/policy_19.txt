Policy ID: POL-019
Policy Name: Mobile Device Policy

Purpose:
This document defines the organization's mobile device policy.

Policy Rules:
Employees accessing company data on mobile devices must enable device encryption and screen locking.

Exceptions:
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to IT within 1 hour.

Responsibility:
The relevant department is responsible for implementing
and monitoring this policy.

Review:
This policy must be reviewed annually.
