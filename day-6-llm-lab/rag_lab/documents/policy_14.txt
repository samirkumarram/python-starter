Policy ID: POL-014
Policy Name: Business Continuity Policy

Purpose:
This document defines the organization's business continuity policy.

Policy Rules:
Critical business applications must be restored within 4 hours of a major outage. The disaster recovery plan must be tested annually.

Exceptions:
The business continuity manager can declare a disaster.

Responsibility:
The relevant department is responsible for implementing
and monitoring this policy.

Review:
This policy must be reviewed annually.
