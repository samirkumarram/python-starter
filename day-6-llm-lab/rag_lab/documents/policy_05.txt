Policy ID: POL-005
Policy Name: Employee Attendance Policy

Purpose:
This document defines the organization's employee attendance policy.

Policy Rules:
Employees must complete 8 working hours daily. Attendance must be recorded using the official attendance system.

Exceptions:
Approved business travel is exempt from office check-in.

Responsibility:
The relevant department is responsible for implementing
and monitoring this policy.

Review:
This policy must be reviewed annually.
