Policy ID: POL-001
Policy Name: Leave Policy

Purpose:
This document defines the organization's leave policy.

Policy Rules:
Employees receive 20 days of paid leave annually. Leave requests must be submitted to the manager at least 3 working days in advance.

Exceptions:
Emergency leave can be approved by the HR manager.

Responsibility:
The relevant department is responsible for implementing
and monitoring this policy.

Review:
This policy must be reviewed annually.
