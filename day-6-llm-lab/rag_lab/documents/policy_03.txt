Policy ID: POL-003
Policy Name: Data Security Policy

Purpose:
This document defines the organization's data security policy.

Policy Rules:
Employees must use strong passwords with at least 12 characters. Passwords must be changed every 90 days.

Exceptions:
Security administrators can enforce immediate password resets.

Responsibility:
The relevant department is responsible for implementing
and monitoring this policy.

Review:
This policy must be reviewed annually.
