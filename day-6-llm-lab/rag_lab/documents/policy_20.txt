Policy ID: POL-020
Policy Name: Employee Exit Policy

Purpose:
This document defines the organization's employee exit policy.

Policy Rules:
Employees leaving the company must return company assets on their last working day. IT must revoke system access within 24 hours of departure.

Exceptions:
HR can request immediate access revocation.

Responsibility:
The relevant department is responsible for implementing
and monitoring this policy.

Review:
This policy must be reviewed annually.
