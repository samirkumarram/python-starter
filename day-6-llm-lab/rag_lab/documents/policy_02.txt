Policy ID: POL-002
Policy Name: Work From Home Policy

Purpose:
This document defines the organization's work from home policy.

Policy Rules:
Employees can work from home up to 2 days per week. Requests must be approved by the reporting manager.

Exceptions:
Employees handling restricted office equipment must work from the office.

Responsibility:
The relevant department is responsible for implementing
and monitoring this policy.

Review:
This policy must be reviewed annually.
