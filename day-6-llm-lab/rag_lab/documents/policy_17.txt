Policy ID: POL-017
Policy Name: Expense Reimbursement Policy

Purpose:
This document defines the organization's expense reimbursement policy.

Policy Rules:
Employees can claim approved business travel expenses within 30 days. Receipts are required for expenses above 500 rupees.

Exceptions:
Exceptions require finance department approval.

Responsibility:
The relevant department is responsible for implementing
and monitoring this policy.

Review:
This policy must be reviewed annually.
